🐺 Privacy Policy — WolfieMD

Automated Utility Bot & Independent Media Gateway

Version 4.1.0 Updated: Nov 28, 2026 Author: The_Flynner

🛡️ 1. Information We Do Not Collect

WolfieMD ("we", "our", or "us") is engineered as a personal utility tool. We do not collect, harvest, store, sell, or distribute any personally identifiable information (PII) of users permanently.

⚙️ 2. How Data is Processed

  • Real-Time Execution: Commands, public media links (such as Pinterest URLs), and search queries are processed strictly in real-time solely to fulfill your explicit request.
  • Temporary Ephemeral Cache: Downloaded media or search previews may be cached temporarily on local storage for fast delivery and are automatically purged within 10 minutes.

🌐 3. Third-Party Services & APIs

Our application interacts with official and public third-party APIs (including the Pinterest REST API) strictly to retrieve public content requested by the user. We fully respect platform Terms of Service, rate limits, and intellectual property rights. No third-party tracking cookies are used.
